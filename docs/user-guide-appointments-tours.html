1. Getting started

Signing in

An admin creates your account and gives you an email and password — staff cannot sign up on their own. Open the app, enter your email and password and sign in. Remember me is ticked by default so you don't have to type them again next time; untick it on a shared device.

Language

The app is available in Vietnamese and English. To switch: on a computer, open the account menu at the bottom of the left sidebar; on a phone, tap Menu in the bottom tab bar → Language → English. This guide uses the English labels.

Who does what

The admin assigns a department to every account. Your department decides what you see in Tours:

Customer Care

Books appointments. Creates a tour when a customer comes in for a treatment. Sees the table of all tours in the month.

Technician

Takes tours, fills in the service detail and bill, takes before/after photos. Views their own tours and earnings by month.

Admin

Reviews and confirms tours, assigns/changes technicians, exports Excel and prints tour slips.

Not seeing the right screen? (e.g. a technician opens Tours but there is no “Waiting” tab) — your account has no department yet. Ask the admin to set it under Users.

Navigation

  • On a computer: the menu is in the left sidebar — Customers, Appointments, Tours.
  • On a phone: the tab bar at the bottom — Customers · Appointments · Tours · Menu. Menu holds your account, dark mode, language and Sign out.

Install the app on your phone (recommended for technicians)

  • iPhone (Safari): open the app → tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): open the app → tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).

General tips

  • After each action a small message appears at the top: e.g. “Saved” (success) or a red error message. If you see an error, read it — it usually says exactly what is wrong.
  • Deleting always asks for confirmation first.
  • On a phone, tables automatically switch to a card list that is easier to read.
  • A customer must already exist under Customers before you can pick them for an appointment or a tour.

2. Appointments

What are appointments for?

Appointments are the log of when customers are scheduled to visit the clinic. Each appointment tells you:

  • Who is coming, when (date & time), for which service, and which Customer Care staff is in charge,
  • And most importantly: whether the customer has arrived (the “Clinic confirmation” column).

This way the whole team knows who is coming today, who hasn't arrived yet and needs a reminder call, and reports can count appointments and arrivals.

Appointments do NOT record money or technicians. The bill, payment, technician and before/after photos all live in Tours (sections 3–6). When a customer comes in for a treatment: mark them Arrived in Appointments, then create a Tour for them.

Add a new appointment

  1. Go to Appointments → click Add appointment at the top right. A form slides in from the right.
  2. Customer name (required): type a name or phone number to search, then pick the customer. Not found → create the customer under Customers first.
  3. Appointment date & time (required): when the customer will come.
  4. Clinic confirmation: leave it at Not arrived. Only switch to Arrived if the customer is already here.
  5. Services: pick one or more services the customer is booked for.
  6. Customer care: the CC staff in charge (usually you).
  7. Note: anything to remember (what the customer wants, deposit paid, who they come with…).
  8. Click Add appointment at the bottom of the form to save.

4. Creating a tour (Customer Care)

Create a tour when the customer has arrived and is about to be treated — so a technician can take it.

  1. Go to Tours → click + New tour.
  2. Date: today by default; only change it when entering a tour for another day.
  3. Customer (required): type a name or phone number to search.
  4. Service (required): the service the customer is getting (e.g. brows, lips…).
  5. Bill (optional): if you already know how much the customer pays, enter it now so the technician doesn't have to.
  6. Note: what the technician should know beforehand (sensitive customer, special request…). Technicians can read it right on the waiting card.
  7. Click New tour. The tour appears in the Waiting tab on every technician's phone.

5. Taking a tour, filling in details, taking photos (Technician)

Technicians use the app on their phone. Open the Tours tab at the bottom — it has 2 tabs: Waiting and My tours.

Step 1 — Take the tour

  1. Open the Waiting tab. The number in brackets is how many tours are waiting. Each card shows the customer, date, service and the CC's note.
  2. Find the customer you are about to treat → tap Take tour.
  3. The app shows “Tour taken”, switches to My tours and opens the tour so you can continue filling it in.

If two people tap at the same time, only the first one gets the tour. The other sees “This tour was already taken” — nothing else to do.

Took the wrong tour? Open it → tap Return tour → confirm. It goes back to the Waiting list for someone else. You can only return a tour that the admin has not confirmed yet.

6. Viewing your tours and earnings (Technician)

Go to Tours → My tours tab. It only shows tours you have taken, month by month.

  1. Pick the month in the month box at the top (current month by default).
  2. Check the 3 figures:
    • Tours — how many tours you did this month.
    • Unconfirmed — tours the admin hasn't approved yet.
    • Received — your total for the month; the small line “Confirmed: …” is the part already approved.
  3. Browse the cards: customer, date · service, status, service detail, photo count and received amount for each tour.
  4. Tap a card to open and edit it (if not confirmed). Tap the photo icon on a card to quickly view before / after / extra photos — tap a photo to enlarge, swipe to see the next one.

7. Admin tasks

  • Confirm a tour: open the tour (or ⋮ at the end of the row) → ✓ Confirm. The tour needs a technician, service detail and bill. Confirmed by mistake → open the tour → Unconfirm.
  • Assign / change technician: the Technician field in the tour details (when a technician forgot to take the tour, or the wrong person took it).
  • Group by technician, Export Excel and Tour slip (PDF): export the currently filtered rows of the month — one slip page per technician with a signature area.
  • Admins can edit and delete any tour, including confirmed ones.

FAQ

Can't find the customer when booking / creating a tour?
The customer isn't in the system yet. Create them under Customers first, then come back. Try searching by phone number instead of name.
Error: “This tour was already taken”.
Another technician took it a few seconds before you. Reopen the Tours tab (or reload the page) to refresh the list and take another tour.
Error: “Tour is confirmed — only admins can edit”.
The tour has been approved and locked. Ask the admin to edit it or unconfirm it.
Error: “Technician, service detail and bill are required before confirming”.
The technician in charge needs to open the tour, fill in the service detail and bill, tap Save, then the admin confirms again.
A technician has no “Waiting” tab, or sees “Only technicians can take tours”.
The account isn't set to the Technician department. Ask the admin to set it under Users.
Tapping “Take photo” doesn't open the camera.
Allow the browser / app to use the camera in your phone settings. If it still doesn't work, take the photo with the normal camera and add it through Extra photos / bill.
What's the difference between an Appointment and a Tour?
An Appointment = when the customer is scheduled to come and whether they've arrived. A Tour = the actual treatment: who did it, bill, photos, commission. A customer who comes in for treatment → 1 appointment (Arrived) and 1 tour.
