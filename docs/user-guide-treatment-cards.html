1. How it works — who does what

A treatment card belongs to one customer and holds one or more services from the price list. Each card has its own number (TLT00001…). All money is recorded on the card — the deposit, every payment and every refund. Tours (treatment sessions) are always created from a card, so the card shows the customer's whole treatment journey.

StepWhatWhere / how
1–2Sell the cardSales Management → cart icon on an appointment whose status is Arrived, In progress or Done. The deposit is applied automatically. See section 2.
3Take paymentOpen the card → Take payment. Cash, bank transfer or card, with a photo of the bill / transfer. See section 4.
4Create toursOpen the card → New tour. Pick the date, the services and the tour Type. See section 6.
5–7Master & AdminMaster takes the tour in Tours → Waiting, does the treatment and uploads photos. Admin confirms the tour.
8Follow-upTelesales calls customers who are due for a laser check or retouch: Customers → Treatment follow-ups. See section 7.

2. Selling a card in Sales Management

The appointment table in Sales Management has a Treatment card column. Before a card exists you see a cart icon; after it is sold you see the card number.

Steps

  1. Set the appointment status to Arrived (or In progress / Done). The cart icon becomes active.
  2. Click the cart. The drawer Sell treatment card · <name> opens.
  3. Left side: tick the services the customer buys — the whole price list is shown by group, like the paper price sheet.
  4. Right side: for each ticked service, check the Sale price (IDR) and the number of Sessions (e.g. a full laser treatment = 5 sessions).
  5. Check the totals at the bottom, add a Note if needed, then click Create card.
  • Discounts: type a lower sale price. The drawer then shows List price total and Discount / voucher automatically. The list price itself is not changed.
  • Deposit: the customer's unused deposit is applied automatically (never more than the card value). A deposit is only used once — a second card gets only what is left.
  • One card per appointment. If the customer buys more later, open the card and use Add service instead of selling a new card.
  • After the card is created the customer's status changes to Purchased automatically.

3. Money on a card

Every card shows three numbers. They are calculated by the system — you never type them.

Worked example

The customer buys Ombre (Rp 6.000.000) and Korea Ink (Rp 2.500.000) = Rp 8.500.000, gets a Rp 500.000 voucher, has already paid a Rp 200.000 deposit, then pays Rp 7.800.000 by card:

Money rules

  • The deposit is applied once. It never exceeds the card value. If a card is cancelled, its deposit becomes available for another card.
  • Refunding a service lowers the card value. The system suggests price × unused sessions ÷ total sessions; you can change it (up to the sale price).
  • Giving money back to the customer = a payment with a negative amount.
  • Payments can't be edited. A wrong payment can only be deleted by an Admin.

How reports read it

Cancelled cards are not counted anywhere.

4. Card details: payments, refunds, sessions

Open a card by clicking its number in Sales Management or any row on the Treatment cards page. Click the customer's name at the top to open the customer profile (360°).

Services

  • Used x/y sessions = tours created ÷ sessions bought. Ink, Grandmaster fees and products do not create tours, so they show no sessions.
  • Add service — opens the same price-list drawer as selling; the new services are added to this card.
  • Edit sessions (pencil) — change the number of sessions. It can't go below the sessions already used.
  • Refund service (arrow) — enter the refund amount (suggested automatically) and a reason. The service is struck through and can't get new tours.

Payments

  1. Click Take payment.
  2. Enter the Amount (IDR). Use a negative amount to give money back.
  3. Choose Cash, Bank transfer or Card, add a note.
  4. Add Photos (bill / transfer) — always attach proof.
  5. Click Take payment again to save. Date and staff name are recorded automatically.

Tour journey & activity log

  • Tour journey lists every tour of the card by date with type, Master and status. Click a tour to open it (photos included).
  • Activity log → Show: who did what and when — card created, services added, sessions changed, refunds, payments, tours created / changed / confirmed.

Cancel card is for Admin only, and only when the card has no tours and the payments add up to 0 (refund first with a negative payment). A cancelled card can't be restored.

Useful filters

  • Sale date — the 4 figures at the top follow the filter, e.g. this month's sales.
  • Status → Balance due — customers who still owe money.
  • Created by — cards sold by one consultant.
  • Cancelled cards only appear when you filter Cancelled.

6. Creating tours from a card & commission

A tour is one treatment session done by a Master. Tours can only be created from a card — the customer, service and price come from the card, so nothing has to be typed twice.

Steps

  1. Open the card → New tour.
  2. Pick the Date (today by default).
  3. Tick the services done in this visit. Services due for a check / retouch are ticked for you.
  4. Check the Type (suggested automatically) and add a note if needed.
  5. Click New tour. The tours appear in Tours → Waiting for the Masters.

Tour types

TypeUse for
NewFirst full treatment of the service
Retouch nTouch-up after a New treatment
Laser nRemoval of old brow / eyeliner work
Plasma nRemoval of old lip colour

The number (Retouch 1, 2…) is counted automatically. When all sessions are used, only an Admin can add another tour.

The Bill shown on a tour is the service's sale price on the card. It is read-only — money is always handled on the card, never on the tour.

7. Treatment follow-ups

Menu Customers → Treatment follow-ups is the Telesales call list. The system works it out from each card's tours — nobody has to create reminders.

Reading the list

ColumnShows
DueDue date + tag: n days overdue, Today, n days left
TaskCheck Laser / Check Plasma, or Retouch n. "Booked …" if an appointment is already made
Last tourDate and type of the last tour
TelesalesThe Telesales in charge of the customer, or Unassigned
ContactCall · SMAX chat (or WhatsApp)

Daily routine for Telesales

  1. Open Treatment follow-ups. The list shows your customers and the range To call (overdue + today).
  2. Contact each customer with Call or SMAX chat / WhatsApp.
  3. When the customer agrees, create the appointment as usual. The row moves to Booked.
  4. Use Next 7 days to plan ahead, All to see everything.

The list contains no prices or payments. Click a customer's name to open the profile and see their history.

Tip for Sales: if a laser check is OK earlier than planned and the customer needs no more laser, reduce the laser sessions to the number already used (Edit sessions). The check then disappears from the list.

8. Printing the card for the customer to sign

  1. Open the card and check that services, sessions and payments are correct.
  2. Click Print card (PDF) at the bottom of the drawer.
  3. In the print window choose the printer — or Save as PDF to send the file to the customer via WhatsApp.
  4. Ask the customer to sign; the Beauty consultant signs next to it.

What is on the printout

  • Card number, date, customer name, phone, address and Beauty consultant (the staff member who created the card).
  • Services with category, sessions used / total and price. Refunded services are struck through with the refund amount.
  • All payments (date, method, amount) and the card note.
  • Totals: Subtotal · Total payment · Deposit (DP) · Paid amount · Balance due — the same lines as the paper price sheet.

Print again after each new payment or added service so the customer always has the latest version.

FAQ

The customer wants a discount.
Type a lower sale price when selling. The discount shows as "Discount / voucher".

The customer pays in instalments.
Take one payment each time. Balance due updates automatically.

I entered a payment by mistake.
You can't edit it. Ask an Admin to delete it, then enter the correct one.

The customer stops the treatment.
Refund the service (amount suggested from unused sessions), then take a negative payment for the money returned.

The customer buys another service on a later visit.
Open the same card → Add service, and take the payment.

Who changed this card?
Open the card → Activity log → Show.
